====================================================
BUSINESS OPERATIONS
====================================================

The platform must support real-world business operations beyond simply taking payments.

TAXES
Support international tax architecture.
Do not hardcode tax rates.
Support Stripe Tax or an equivalent tax provider where appropriate.

CUSTOMS
Products must support:
- HS/tariff code
- country of origin
- customs description
- customs value
- weight
- dimensions

RETURNS
Implement a complete return workflow:
customer request
→ admin review
→ approved/denied
→ return received
→ refund/exchange
→ inventory adjustment

Support:
- partial returns
- exchanges
- damaged products
- wrong products
- refund status

SHIPPING RATES
Admin-configurable shipping zones and rates.
Support:
- free shipping
- flat rate
- expedited shipping
- country-specific shipping
- carrier-calculated rates where supported

INVENTORY RESERVATIONS
Prevent overselling during concurrent checkout.

Use inventory reservations with expiration.

AVAILABLE
→ RESERVED
→ SOLD

Expired reservations return to AVAILABLE.

FRAUD
Integrate with Stripe's fraud/risk capabilities.
Flag suspicious orders for manual review.

Do not automatically fulfill orders that require manual review.

REVIEWS
Support verified-purchase reviews.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT
Allow customers to contact support from an order.
Automatically attach relevant order and tracking information.

REPORTING
Add:
- gross revenue
- net revenue
- refunds
- discounts
- shipping revenue
- taxes
- average order value
- conversion rate
- repeat purchase rate

DATA EXPORT
Admin can export:
- orders
- customers
- inventory
- products
- sales

OBSERVABILITY
Implement application error monitoring and integration health monitoring.

Show admin connection/status indicators for:
- PostgreSQL
- Stripe
- Clerk
- Resend
- ShipStation

BACKUPS
Implement/enable automated database backup strategy and document restoration procedure.

LEGAL
Create configurable pages for:
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service
- Shipping Policy
- Return/Refund Policy
- Cookie Policy
- Accessibility
