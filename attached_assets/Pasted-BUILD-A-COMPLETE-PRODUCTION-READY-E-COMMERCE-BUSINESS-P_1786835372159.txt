BUILD A COMPLETE PRODUCTION-READY E-COMMERCE BUSINESS PLATFORM

I want you to build a complete, production-quality, full-stack e-commerce platform for a company that sells anime/movie-themed lifting straps, wristbands, belts, and other fitness accessories worldwide.

THIS IS NOT JUST A WEBSITE.

It must be both:

1. A world-class cinematic e-commerce storefront for customers
2. A complete business operating system/admin portal for the owner

The customer-facing website should feel like a combination of:

- AAA anime/game experience
- Cinematic movie website
- Premium fitness brand
- High-end streetwear brand
- Nike-level product campaign
- Luxury e-commerce
- Awwwards-level interactive website

The admin portal should feel completely different:

- Extremely simple
- Extremely fast
- Extremely organized
- Extremely easy to understand
- Mobile friendly
- Designed so a non-technical business owner can run the company without touching code

The ultimate goal is:

CUSTOMER:
"This is one of the coolest websites I've ever seen."

OWNER:
"I can run my entire company from here."

==================================================
1. IMPORTANT COPYRIGHT REQUIREMENT
==================================================

The company sells anime/movie-themed products.

Do NOT use copyrighted anime/movie characters, footage, logos, music, or artwork unless the company has the appropriate licenses.

The system must support licensed artwork when available.

For demo/placeholder visuals, use ORIGINAL anime-inspired artwork rather than copying existing copyrighted characters.

==================================================
2. PRODUCT DESIGN PDF
==================================================

The business owner has provided a PDF containing the designs for the company's belts/straps.

The PDF is an important source asset.

When the PDF is available in the project:

1. Inspect the PDF.
2. Identify every unique product/design.
3. Identify variants and colorways.
4. Extract product names where provided.
5. Extract relevant product information where available.
6. Organize the designs into a product catalog.
7. Create DRAFT product records from the designs.
8. Allow the admin to review and edit everything.
9. NEVER automatically publish products without admin approval.
10. NEVER invent product designs or specifications that aren't supported by the provided materials.

Create an admin feature:

ADMIN → PRODUCT DESIGN IMPORT

Workflow:

UPLOAD PDF
↓
ANALYZE PDF
↓
IDENTIFY DESIGNS
↓
CREATE DRAFT PRODUCTS
↓
ADMIN REVIEWS
↓
ADMIN EDITS
↓
PUBLISH

If the PDF cannot be reliably interpreted automatically, create a manual review interface rather than guessing.
